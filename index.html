Steps to run

Tab 1 · Flood Response

  1. Set the Flood level slider (1 to 12 m). A higher level floods more area.
  2. Click ▶ Trigger flood alert. The four steps on the left run in order: flood analysis, suitable landing sites, route analysis, sharing.
  3. Watch the map. Blue is flood water, green marks landing sites and open roads, orange is road to a landing site, cyan is helicopter airlift, red marks towns at risk or cut off. The Live Accessibility panel and Event log on the right update as it runs.
  4. Once step 3 is done, click ⏩ Simulate rising river to watch routes re-plan as the water rises. You can also drag the slider to re-plan manually.
  5. Click 📤 Share to pick recipients, edit the alert message, copy it, open it in WhatsApp or email, or export the map (PNG) and accessibility table (CSV).
  6. ⏸ Pause (or the Space bar) freezes the simulation. Reset starts over.

Tab 2 · GeoAI Assistant

  • Click a suggestion chip or type a question in plain language, for example Show wards with high heat and low tree cover, then press Ask or Enter.
  • The district map highlights the priority wards and lists the layers used.
  • Run the flood simulation first, then ask which towns are cut off? to query the live flood plan.

Note: all data is illustrative and “Send alert” only simulates delivery. Press Esc to close this window.
